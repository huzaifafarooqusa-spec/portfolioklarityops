Adoption

A great system nobody uses is worthless.

Most consultants build the system and leave. We train every person two ways: at the right fluency level, and for the seat they actually sit in. Until the team owns the system on their own.

By fluency level

Beginner

Daily confidence. Find tasks, update status, comment, and know where things live.

Intermediate

Fluent operator. Custom fields, filters, views, dependencies, and following SOPs correctly.

Advanced

System builder. Build and maintain projects, run dashboards and reporting for their area.

Power User

Internal champion. Automations, templates, troubleshooting, and training others.

Admin / Champion

Governance, template management, keeping the system clean as it scales.

By role

End User

How to do daily work cleanly inside the system. Find tasks, update status, and keep things moving.

Team Lead
